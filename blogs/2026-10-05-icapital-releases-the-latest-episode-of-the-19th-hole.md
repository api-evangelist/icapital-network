NEW YORK, NY — iCapital 1, the global fintech company shaping the future of investing, today released a new episode of its award-winning interview series, The 19th Hole: Beyond the Green , hosted by iCapital Chairman and CEO Lawrence Calcano . The new episode features a thought-provoking conversation with Jenny Johnson , President and CEO of Franklin Templeton. In this episode, Jenny Johnson reflects on the leadership principles that have guided Franklin Templeton's transformation, her commitment to innovation, and her belief that enduring success is built on a strong and adaptable culture.
