NEW YORK, NY — iCapital 1 , the global fintech company shaping the future of investing, today released a new episode of its interview series, The 19th Hole: Beyond the Green , hosted by iCapital Chairman and CEO Lawrence Calcano . The new episode features a wide-ranging conversation with Marc Rowan , Co-Founder, CEO, and Chair of Apollo Global Management. The conversation explores how a firm that has reshaped the private markets landscape thinks about building culture, developing leaders, and navigating the ongoing convergence of public and private markets.
