ZURICH, PARIS— Tikehau Capital, a global alternative asset manager, today announced the expansion of its partnership with iCapital 1 , the global fintech company shaping the future of investing, by making a Tikehau Capital European private credit strategy available through iCapital Marketplace. The addition broadens the platform’s offering of evergreen and ELTIF 2.0 investment opportunities for wealth managers across eligible EMEA markets. The Luxembourg-domiciled, euro-denominated, evergreen strategy invests primarily in first-lien senior secured debt issued by European companies.
